Key Takeaways

Perfect Practice ranks #147 of 215 golf brands on the DORMIED Index for Jun 2026, with a Demand Index score of 0.7, down 15.8% month over month. Its strongest single market is Australia, where it ranks #28. Perfect Practice is a Tech & Training Aids brand, founded in 2017, based in California, USA.

Perfect Practice has become synonymous with at-home putting training since launching in 2017 from California. The company's flagship product, the Perfect Practice Putting Mat, earned widespread adoption by replicating real green speeds and ball roll characteristics in a format designed for home or office use. Unlike cheaper alternatives that offer artificial feedback, Perfect Practice mats use materials engineered to mimic actual putting surfaces, giving golfers realistic distance control practice indoors. The brand expanded beyond putting mats to include alignment tools, training aids, and chipping nets, but its core identity remains rooted in serious practice equipment for players looking to lower scores through short-game improvement. Perfect Practice capitalized on the surge in home golf training during the pandemic, building a direct-to-consumer business model supported by strong social proof from teaching professionals and amateur players alike. The company's products frequently appear in golf instruction content across YouTube and Instagram, where instructors use them as teaching tools. While the brand lacks tour presence or professional endorsements, its credibility comes from the quality of its training surfaces and the endorsement of club fitters and coaches who recommend them to students. Perfect Practice represents a shift in training aid marketing, less gimmick, more fundamental skill-building, targeting golfers who understand that putting accounts for roughly 40% of their score and are willing to invest in deliberate practice at home.
