Khalhon

Founded 2021 · Seoul, South Korea

Apparel & Footwear
#165
Global Rank
View Index →

Khalhon launched in 2021 as a Seoul-based golf apparel brand reimagining American prep style through a Korean design lens. The brand blends performance fabrics with everyday versatility, creating pieces that work on the course but are intended to be worn beyond the golf environment. Khalhon's aesthetic draws from classic American sportswear, polos, sweaters, outerwear, but reinterprets these staples with modern fits, subtle branding, and design details that appeal to younger, fashion-conscious golfers. The brand emerged during a period of rapid growth in Korean golf culture and amid a broader global trend of golf apparel becoming more lifestyle-oriented. Khalhon targets millennials and Gen Z golfers who view their golf wardrobe as part of a larger personal style rather than sport-specific attire. The brand operates primarily direct-to-consumer online and has built awareness through social media and collaborations with influencers and content creators in the golf space. Khalhon competes in an increasingly crowded market of lifestyle golf brands, including Malbon, Greyson, and others that have similarly blurred the line between performance apparel and streetwear. The brand differentiates through its Korean design perspective and its focus on versatility, pieces that transition seamlessly from the course to daily life. Khalhon's growth strategy relies on cultural momentum within the younger golf demographic and the continued acceptance of golf apparel as legitimate lifestyle fashion. The brand's challenge is scaling distribution and awareness beyond early adopters while maintaining its design identity and premium positioning. Khalhon's success will likely depend on whether it can build lasting brand equity in a category where trends shift quickly and where new entrants constantly compete for attention.
