Vice Golf

Founded 2012 · Munich, Germany

Clubs & Balls
#9
Global Rank
View Index →

Vice Golf disrupted the golf ball market when it launched in Munich in 2012, pioneering a direct-to-consumer model that bypassed traditional retail and undercut established brands by 30-50% while claiming comparable performance. Founded by Rainer Stoeckl and Ingo Duellmann, Vice built its business on a simple premise: eliminate retail middlemen, sell online in bulk quantities, and invest savings into product quality and aggressive marketing. The brand's golf balls, including the Pro, Pro Plus, Pro Soft, and Tour models, target different swing speeds and player preferences, with Vice claiming tour-level construction and urethane covers at prices significantly below Titleist, Callaway, and TaylorMade equivalents. Vice's bold, Instagram-friendly branding and viral marketing campaigns helped build a global following, particularly among younger golfers comfortable with online purchasing and skeptical of traditional golf industry pricing. The company expanded beyond balls into apparel, headcovers, and accessories, maintaining its irreverent, design-forward aesthetic across categories. Vice has pursued tour validation aggressively, signing professional players and sponsoring events to combat skepticism about direct-to-consumer golf ball quality. The brand's success forced established manufacturers to respond with their own direct programs and value-oriented ball lines, fundamentally changing golf ball distribution and pricing dynamics. Vice now operates globally with regional operations in North America, Europe, and Asia, selling millions of balls annually. The company's trajectory illustrates how digital-native brands can challenge entrenched players in equipment categories previously dominated by legacy manufacturers with massive R&D budgets and retail relationships. Vice's ongoing challenge involves maintaining pricing advantage and brand differentiation as established competitors adapt and new direct-to-consumer entrants continue emerging.
