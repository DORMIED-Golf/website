Sun Day Red

Founded 2024 · San Clemente USA

Apparel & Footwear
#8
Global Rank
View Index →

Sun Day Red launched in early 2024 as Tiger Woods' first signature apparel brand following the end of his Nike partnership, created in collaboration with TaylorMade Golf Company. The brand name references Woods' iconic ritual of wearing a red shirt on Sundays throughout his career, a visual signature so embedded in golf culture that it became synonymous with his dominance. Sun Day Red positions itself as a premium lifestyle and performance apparel brand, blending technical golf wear with elevated design for athletes and consumers who identify with Woods' legacy. The line includes polos, outerwear, pants, and accessories, all built around performance fabrics and a refined, minimalist aesthetic that reflects Woods' personal style. TaylorMade's infrastructure and distribution network give Sun Day Red immediate access to retail channels and marketing resources that most athlete-founded brands lack. The brand targets affluent golfers and Woods fans willing to pay luxury pricing for apparel connected to one of sport's most iconic figures. Early product drops sold quickly, driven by Woods' announcement and pent-up demand following his exit from Nike. Sun Day Red faces the challenge of maintaining relevance as Woods' playing career winds down, but the brand's positioning suggests it aims to transcend his on-course presence and build a standalone lifestyle identity. The apparel market is crowded with premium golf brands, but few carry the cultural weight of Woods' personal brand. Sun Day Red's success will depend on whether it can translate that equity into sustained product innovation and desirability beyond the initial hype cycle, and whether consumers view it as a genuine performance brand or primarily a nostalgia play.

Key Moments

The product, marketing, culture, and on-course moments that moved search interest in Sun Day Red in a given month.

May 2026
  • Tiger Woods skipped his own May 12 court date in the DUI case, with his attorney appearing instead as prescription drug records were handed over to prosecutors, keeping the brand's founder in a damaging news cycle all month.
  • Woods confirmed he will miss all four 2026 majors, leaving the brand with no tour-visible ambassador for the sport's biggest stages during peak golf season.
  • Sun Day Red launched an Archive Sale at 30% off select styles on May 20, a markdown move that typically signals inventory pressure and pulls against premium brand positioning.
  • The first full women's collection launched into a crowded market without a competitive platform to anchor attention, arriving two years after the men's line with no tour athlete driving awareness.
Mar 2026
  • Spring Traditions Collection launched March 26, featuring Augusta-inspired designs and limited-edition footwear timed ahead of the Masters
  • Tiger Woods was arrested for DUI on March 27 after a rollover crash in Florida, with his Cosmic Blue Review polo from the arrest selling out completely within days
  • Woods made his first TGL appearance of the season on March 24 for Jupiter Links Golf Club, drawing nearly 1 million viewers according to ESPN
Feb 2026
  • The Pioneer Willow golf shoe launched on February 26, along with multiple other significant product drops including the Presidio spikeless shoe releasing February 17.
  • Tommy Fleetwood, the 2025 FedExCup champion and TaylorMade staffer, wore Sun Day Red's Rhythm Stripe Cashmere Crew at the Genesis Invitational following his split from Nike after a 16-year partnership, generating widespread media coverage as the country's top golfer tested Tiger's brand publicly.
Jan 2026
  • Taiwan became the brand's first international market outside North America through a partnership with EMERS, launching a pop-up store from January 13 to March 1 with exclusive access to the collection and a limited-edition 3D Logo Hoodie.
  • Tiger Woods announced the Sun Day Red Presidio spikeless golf shoe, featuring an athletic-style design with a unique traction pattern, available starting in February.
  • David Abeles, CEO of both Sun Day Red and TaylorMade, confirmed at the PGA Show that revenue growth has outpaced early benchmarks, with the brand surpassing its initial financial targets.
