What Should You Know About Radry Golf?

Radry Golf ranks #184 of 215 golf brands on the DORMIED Index for Aug 2026, with a Demand Index score of 0.1, up +22.9% month over month. Its strongest single market is Japan, where it ranks #112. Radry Golf is an Apparel & Footwear brand, founded in 2018, based in Austin, Texas, USA.

Radry Golf describes itself as a never ending art project disguised as a golf brand, and the trajectory backs that up more than most brand statements do. Tony Knapton spent years building Routine Baseball into a recognised name in that sport, with adidas and MLB player collaborations, then left in 2018 to start Radry. It launched with a small capsule and then went quiet for three years while Knapton took the creative director role at Random Golf Club, which is itself already on the Index. He returned to Radry full time in January 2023. The output spans apparel, headcovers, towels, gloves, resin figurines and actual art, sold through the brand's own Art and Objects categories alongside the clothing, and it is represented by White Dot Market. A Radry's Golf & Social Club is planned for Sheboygan, Wisconsin. The RGC connection matters for reading the data: Knapton helped shape the visual language of one of golf's defining vibe brands, and Radry is what he does with that when nobody else owns the brief.
