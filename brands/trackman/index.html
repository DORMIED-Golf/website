Key Takeaways

Trackman ranks #25 of 215 golf brands on the DORMIED Index for Jun 2026, with a Demand Index score of 13.5, down 18.3% month over month. Its strongest single market is Australia, where it ranks #9. Trackman is a Tech & Training Aids brand, founded in 2003, based in Vedbæk, Denmark.

TrackMan revolutionized golf instruction, club fitting, and equipment development when it introduced radar-based ball flight tracking to the sport in 2003. Founded in Denmark by physicist Fredrik Tuxen, who originally developed radar tracking technology for military applications, TrackMan became the first commercially viable launch monitor to capture complete ball flight data in real outdoor environments rather than relying on camera systems or net-based projections. The company's dual-radar technology simultaneously tracks club delivery and ball flight, measuring over 20 parameters including spin rates, launch angles, carry distances, and club path with precision that established TrackMan as the industry standard for equipment testing and professional fitting. Every major equipment manufacturer uses TrackMan for R&D validation and tour support, while nearly all elite instructors and fitting centers consider the units essential infrastructure despite pricing around $20,000-25,000 for current models. The technology's influence extends beyond measurement, TrackMan's data outputs fundamentally changed how golf instruction and club fitting are conducted, shifting emphasis from subjective feel toward quantified performance optimization. Tour professionals routinely practice with TrackMan feedback, and the company's combine testing has created standardized performance benchmarks across skill levels. TrackMan also powers indoor simulator experiences and remote coaching through software that connects instructors with students globally. The brand faces increasing competition from photometric systems like Foresight GC Quad and more affordable radar options from FlightScope, but maintains market leadership through continuous software development, integration partnerships, and entrenched adoption among tour operations and premium fitting studios. TrackMan's business model has evolved toward software subscriptions and simulator experiences alongside hardware sales, with the company expanding into entertainment golf through partnerships with indoor venues. Industry observers note that TrackMan's precision standards have raised baseline expectations across golf technology, launch monitors that would have seemed remarkable a decade ago now face scrutiny when measurements deviate from TrackMan validation, establishing the Danish company as golf's de facto measurement authority.
