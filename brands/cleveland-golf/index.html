Cleveland Golf

Founded 1979 · Huntington Beach, California, USA

Clubs & Balls
#30
Global Rank
View Index →

Cleveland Golf built its reputation in the short game trenches, becoming synonymous with wedges and forgiveness-focused club design. Founded in 1979 in Huntington Beach, California, the brand established itself as the wedge specialist, introducing the 588 wedge line in the 1980s and pioneering innovations like laser milling and rotex face technology designed to maximize spin and control around greens. For decades, Cleveland wedges were the default choice for players who valued short-game performance over brand prestige, offering tour-level technology at accessible prices. The company's identity has always leaned practical rather than aspirational, Cleveland makes clubs for golfers who care about getting up and down, not what logo is stamped on their equipment. Beyond wedges, Cleveland produces full club sets, including the Launcher driver and iron lines designed for game improvement and maximum forgiveness. The brand's approach prioritizes ease of use and value, targeting mid-handicap golfers who want reliable performance without paying for tour player endorsements or flashy marketing campaigns. Cleveland's ownership history reflects the consolidation waves that reshaped the golf industry. SRI Sports acquired the brand in 2007, and when Sumitomo Rubber Industries sold its international golf business to Dunlop Sports in 2017, Cleveland landed under Dunlop's umbrella alongside Srixon. The brands now operate as sister companies, sharing technology and resources while maintaining distinct market positions, Srixon targets competitive players, Cleveland focuses on value and forgiveness. Cleveland's tour presence has diminished compared to its 1990s peak, when players like Vijay Singh carried Cleveland wedges. Today the brand maintains lower-profile tour relationships, relying more on grassroots credibility and retailer partnerships than big-name endorsements. For golfers who view equipment as tools rather than status symbols, Cleveland remains a trusted choice, proof that performance and value can coexist without sacrificing either. It's the brand for players who'd rather spend money on greens fees than premium upcharges, and in that lane, Cleveland continues delivering exactly what it promises: clubs that work, priced fairly, without the hype.

Who owns Cleveland Golf?

Cleveland Golf is owned by Sumitomo Rubber Industries.

Where is Cleveland Golf based?

Cleveland Golf is based in Huntington Beach, California, United States. It was founded in 1979 by Roger Cleveland.

As of Jun 2026, Cleveland Golf ranks #30 of 175 golf brands on the DORMIED Index with a DI score of 11.0. Its search interest is up 30.5% over the last three months. Its strongest market is France, where it ranks #22.
