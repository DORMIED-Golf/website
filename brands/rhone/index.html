Rhone

Founded 2014 · Stamford, Connecticut, USA

Apparel & Footwear
#159

Rhone entered the performance lifestyle apparel market in 2014 targeting active professionals seeking versatile clothing that performs across workouts, work, and daily life. Founded in Stamford, Connecticut, the brand built its initial reputation on men's activewear featuring technical fabrics and thoughtful design details that prioritized function without sacrificing style. While not originally a golf brand, Rhone's expansion into golf reflects both the sport's growing appeal among its core demographic and the blurring lines between athletic, casual, and golf apparel. The brand's golf-relevant products include polos with moisture-wicking and anti-odor technologies, performance pants with stretch and weather resistance, and layering pieces designed for athletic movement. Rhone's aesthetic sits between traditional golf brands and pure streetwear, offering clean designs with subtle branding that work in country club settings while maintaining modern silhouettes. The company emphasizes fabric technology. proprietary blends with names like GoldFusion™ (anti-odor) and FlexKnit (four-way stretch). positioning itself as a performance brand grounded in material science. Rhone's most significant golf validation came through its partnership as the Exclusive Official On-Course Apparel Partner of the LPGA Tour, outfitting tour officials and creating branded collections. This partnership provides tour-level visibility while aligning with the brand's broader positioning around active, professional audiences. The company operates primarily direct-to-consumer through its website but has expanded into select retail partnerships. Pricing positions Rhone as premium performance wear, with polos typically $88-98 and pants around $128, competing directly with established golf performance brands. The brand appeals to golfers who view their golf wardrobe as part of a cohesive active lifestyle rather than a separate category, seeking pieces that transition seamlessly from morning rounds to afternoon meetings. Rhone represents the trend of athletic lifestyle brands recognizing golf as a growth category while bringing performance credibility and design sensibilities from outside traditional golf industry patterns.
