Key Takeaways

Calliope Golf ranks #193 of 215 golf brands on the DORMIED Index for Jul 2026, with a Demand Index score of 0.1, down 17.9% month over month. Its strongest single market is Australia, where it ranks #110. Calliope Golf is an Apparel & Footwear brand, founded in 2020, based in Spokane Valley, Washington, USA.

Calliope Golf emerged from Spokane Valley, Washington in 2020 with a founding principle that doubled as market critique: women deserve more than the back corner of the pro shop. The brand built its identity around colorful, personality-driven designs and an explicit focus on community, which it calls the Calliope Crew, an approach that positions golf apparel as much about belonging and identity as about what you wear during a round. The aesthetic skews bold, with vibrant prints, playful patterns, and silhouettes designed to stand out rather than blend into traditional golf conservatism. Calliope represents the wave of women's golf brands founded by women who experienced firsthand the industry's historical neglect of female golfers and decided to build the alternative themselves. The community focus isn't just marketing, the brand actively cultivates connection among customers through social media, events, and inclusive messaging that emphasizes golf as accessible and welcoming rather than exclusive and intimidating. Product-wise, Calliope offers the standard women's golf categories, polos, skorts, pants, outerwear, but differentiated through color, pattern, and a design sensibility that feels more aligned with contemporary women's fashion than traditional golf apparel. The brand operates primarily direct-to-consumer, which allows for closer customer relationships and faster response to feedback but limits the pro shop presence that still drives significant golf apparel sales. Calliope competes in an increasingly crowded field of women's golf lifestyle brands, but has carved out space through consistent aesthetic identity and genuine community engagement. As women's golf participation continues growing and as younger, more diverse women enter the sport, brands like Calliope benefit from serving customers who were underserved or ignored by traditional golf retail.
