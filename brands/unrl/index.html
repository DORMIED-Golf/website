Key Takeaways

UNRL ranks #137 of 215 golf brands on the DORMIED Index for Jul 2026, with a Demand Index score of 1.0, up +26.3% month over month. Its strongest single market is United States, where it ranks #120. UNRL is an Apparel & Footwear brand, founded in 2013, based in St. Paul, Minnesota, USA.

UNRL, founded in 2013 and based in St. Paul, Minnesota, built its initial reputation in performance teamwear and athleisure before expanding into golf. The brand's aesthetic leans toward modern minimalism, premium fabrications, and layering systems that blur the line between athletic performance and lifestyle apparel. UNRL's golf presence has grown notably in recent years, aligning with broader trends toward athletic-inspired golf apparel that prioritizes comfort, stretch, and contemporary silhouettes over traditional polo-and-khakis formality. The brand's offerings include performance polos, quarter-zips, joggers, and outerwear designed to function on the course while remaining stylish enough for post-round wear. UNRL emphasizes technical details, four-way stretch fabrics, moisture management, and tailored fits, that appeal to younger golfers and those influenced by streetwear and modern athletic fashion. The brand has cultivated visibility through strategic collaborations and endorsements, though it operates outside the traditional tour-driven marketing model that dominates legacy golf apparel brands. Instead, UNRL targets culture-forward golfers through digital marketing, influencer partnerships, and direct-to-consumer channels. The company's positioning reflects golf's ongoing demographic and stylistic shift, where apparel increasingly reflects broader fashion trends rather than sport-specific conventions. UNRL competes in an increasingly crowded field of brands, from established players like Nike and adidas to newer entrants like Greyson and Metalwood Studio, all vying for golfers who prioritize style and versatility. For UNRL, success depends on maintaining its modern aesthetic credibility while proving that its performance credentials match its visual appeal, a balance that defines the contemporary golf apparel landscape.
