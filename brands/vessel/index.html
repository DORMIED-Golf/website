Key Takeaways

Vessel ranks #55 of 215 golf brands on the DORMIED Index for Jul 2026, with a Demand Index score of 6.0, flat month over month. Its strongest single market is China, where it ranks #22. Vessel is a Bags & Accessories brand, founded in 2011, based in Carlsbad, California, USA.

Vessel revolutionized the golf bag category when it launched in 2011 from Carlsbad, California, applying luxury materials and meticulous construction to a product segment historically dominated by functional but unremarkable designs. Founded by Hunter Swartz, Vessel bags feature premium leather accents, high-grade hardware, and tour-level structural engineering, positioning the brand as the premium alternative to traditional bag manufacturers. Vessel's product line spans cart bags, stand bags, and player bags, all characterized by attention to detail, YKK zippers, microsuede linings, full-grain leather trim, and thoughtful pocket organization. The brand's quiet but significant influence on professional golf comes through its white-label manufacturing for tour players; many professionals use Vessel-made bags that carry their primary sponsor's branding, a testament to the company's construction quality and tour-grade durability. This behind-the-scenes presence has given Vessel credibility among serious golfers while maintaining a relatively low public profile compared to legacy brands like Titleist or TaylorMade. Vessel sells primarily direct-to-consumer and through select premium retailers and country clubs, avoiding mass-market distribution in favor of controlled brand positioning. The bags typically retail between $400 and $600, pricing that reflects the materials and construction while targeting golfers who view their bag as a long-term investment rather than disposable equipment. Vessel's success reflects broader premiumization trends across golf, where discerning consumers increasingly seek products that combine function with craftsmanship and design. The brand has expanded into accessories including headcovers, travel bags, and leather goods, all maintaining the same material quality and aesthetic cohesion that defined its initial bag offerings.
