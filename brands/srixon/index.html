Srixon

Founded 1930 · Kobe, Japan

Clubs & Balls
#16
Global Rank
View Index →

Srixon, a subsidiary of Sumitomo Rubber Industries under the Dunlop Sports umbrella, has operated since 1930 as one of Japan's most technically rigorous golf equipment manufacturers. The brand is best known for its golf balls, particularly the Z-STAR and Z-STAR XV lines, which compete directly with Titleist's Pro V1 family and have gained significant tour adoption, including major championship wins. Srixon balls are engineered in Kobe with a focus on spin control and urethane cover formulations, and the company's FastLayer core technology represents years of proprietary materials research. Beyond balls, Srixon produces a full line of clubs with particular strength in forged irons, the ZX series has become popular among better players seeking soft feel and workability. The brand's woods and wedges also maintain a reputation for quality, though Srixon doesn't command the same market share in clubs as in balls. Tour professionals like Hideki Matsuyama and Brooks Koepka have used Srixon equipment to win majors, providing validation in a market obsessed with tour performance. The brand's marketing budget is modest compared to Titleist or Callaway, which has kept Srixon positioned as a connoisseur's choice rather than a mass-market leader. In recent years, Srixon has invested in custom fitting infrastructure and digital engagement to expand beyond its traditional base of precision-oriented players. The company's Cleveland Golf sister brand handles wedges and putters, allowing Srixon to focus on balls, irons, and woods. As Japanese manufacturing and engineering prowess continue to earn respect in golf, Srixon represents the quiet, technical alternative to more marketing-driven Western brands.

Frequently Asked

Who owns Srixon?

Srixon is owned by Sumitomo Rubber Industries. Sister brands: Cleveland Golf, XXIO.

Where is Srixon based?

Srixon is based in Japan.

Is Srixon a good golf brand?

As of May 2026, Srixon ranks #16 of 175 golf brands on the DORMIED Index with a DI score of 20.1. Its search interest is up 30.7% over the last three months. Its strongest market is Japan, where it ranks #8.

Do any pros play Srixon?
