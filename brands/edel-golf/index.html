Edel Golf

Founded 1996 · Liberty Hill, Texas, USA

Clubs & Balls
#123
Global Rank
View Index →

Edel Golf operates on a premise that challenges conventional equipment design: that every golfer sees alignment differently, and clubs should be built to match individual perception rather than generic standards. Founded in 1996 by David Edel and based in Liberty Hill, Texas, the brand specializes in precision-fit putters, wedges, and irons built around extensive fitting protocols and modular club designs. Edel's fitting process includes visual alignment assessments, stroke analysis, and impact testing to determine not just length and loft but also how a golfer's eyes and brain interpret lines and angles. This philosophy led to the development of torque-balanced putters and wedges with customizable sole grinds, weights, and finishes. The brand's putters, in particular, have gained a cult following among fitters and players who prioritize feel and consistency. Edel offers more than 20 different putter head shapes and an array of hosel configurations, allowing fitters to dial in precise specifications for each golfer. The company's wedge line includes the SMS (Short Metal System) models, which feature modular sole designs that can be adjusted or swapped to suit different turf conditions and swing styles. Edel's approach is boutique and fitting-intensive, making it less accessible than mass-market brands but highly valued among serious players and equipment purists. The brand has maintained independence and a reputation for technical rigor, often appearing in the bags of club professionals and low-handicap amateurs who have been through a detailed fitting process. While Edel lacks the tour presence and marketing reach of larger OEMs, it represents a growing segment of golfers who prioritize customization, data, and precision over brand recognition. In an industry increasingly dominated by technology and marketing, Edel remains stubbornly focused on the fundamentals: fit, feel, and individual performance.
