Graphite Design

Founded 1989 · Chichibu, Saitama, Japan

Tech & Training Aids
#90
Global Rank
View Index →

Graphite Design has been manufacturing premium golf shafts in Japan since 1989, building a reputation for meticulous engineering and tour-level performance that rivals any shaft brand globally. Based in Chichibu, Saitama, the company operates its own manufacturing facilities, controlling the entire production process from raw carbon fiber layup to final assembly, a vertical integration uncommon in the shaft industry and central to Graphite Design's quality consistency. The brand's Tour AD (Tour Authentic Design) line represents its flagship franchise, available in multiple weight classes and flex profiles, each designated by a color-coded series: Tour AD DI, IZ, UB, HD, and others, each engineered with specific kick point locations, torque ratings, and weight distributions to match different swing characteristics and desired ball flights. Tour AD shafts are among the most-played on professional tours worldwide, frequently appearing in the drivers and fairway woods of major championship contenders. Graphite Design's approach emphasizes empirical testing and incremental refinement rather than marketing-driven model cycles, with new releases introduced when genuine technological advances justify them. The brand's reputation rests on stability, consistency between individual shafts, and predictable performance, qualities that matter enormously to tour professionals and serious amateurs making equipment investments based on fitting data. Pricing reflects the premium positioning, with Tour AD shafts typically retailing between $350 and $450, comparable to Fujikura Ventus or Mitsubishi Tensei. Beyond drivers and woods, Graphite Design produces iron shafts and specialty models, though the brand remains most identified with woods where graphite shaft performance differences are most pronounced. Graphite Design operates primarily through authorized dealers, custom club fitters, and OEM partnerships with equipment manufacturers who offer Graphite Design as premium upgrade options. The brand's Japanese heritage and manufacturing location contribute to its mystique, carbon fiber craftsmanship associated with precision and uncompromising standards. In a category crowded with new materials claims and tour validation battles, Graphite Design's positioning is straightforward: decades of proven performance, made in Japan, trusted by the world's best players when the stakes are highest.
