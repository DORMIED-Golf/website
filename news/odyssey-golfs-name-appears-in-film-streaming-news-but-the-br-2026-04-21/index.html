Odyssey Golf's Name Appears in Film Streaming News, But the Brand Itself Remains Silent

Odyssey Golf ranks 78th globally despite its legacy status. The iconic putter brand is generating minimal buzz while competitors gain ground.

By Travis  ·  April 21, 2026  ·  Apparel  ·  2 min read

Image: PR Newswire

A press release about an independent film called "Alex & Mor: A Love Odyssey" has been making rounds, and if you clicked hoping for putter news, you are not alone. The Odyssey in question has nothing to do with the Callaway-owned putter brand that has dominated tour bags for decades. It is a debut feature from filmmaker Tim Glover, streaming on a platform called Mometu. The golf brand Odyssey was not involved.

This kind of name collision happens more often than you would think, and it serves as an unintentional reminder of where Odyssey Golf actually stands in the broader conversation. The brand currently sits at 78th globally in our tracking, with a score of just 2 out of 100. That is not a typo. One of the most recognized names in putting, a brand that has spent years on the bags of major champions, is generating almost no measurable buzz relative to the rest of the market.

The 22 percent month-over-month increase sounds promising until you realize it is movement from near-zero to slightly above near-zero. Odyssey has not released a major product announcement, launched a campaign, or made noise in any of the channels we track. The brand appears to be coasting on legacy recognition while competitors in the putter space push harder for attention.

This is the paradox of heritage brands in golf equipment. Odyssey does not need to introduce itself. Every serious golfer knows the name. But awareness is not the same as relevance, and relevance is not the same as momentum. Younger brands and direct-to-consumer challengers are filling the space that Odyssey seems content to leave empty.

Whether this is strategic patience or quiet stagnation depends on what Callaway has planned for the second half of the year. A brand with this much equity does not stay dormant forever. But right now, the most interesting thing connected to the name Odyssey is a movie about love, not a flatstick.
