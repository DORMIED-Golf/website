Arnie McNair Clothing: Every Piece, Every Price, and Why It Keeps Selling Out

By Adam R.  ·  August 25, 2026  ·  Feature  ·  6 min read

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Quick Answer

Arnie McNair's collection consists of the Arnie Polo and McNair Polo, Washington Shorts, the Vincenzo Cashmere sweater, the AM and George visors, and the Eisenhower belt and wallet, all produced in limited seasonal runs from natural fibres.

Adam R. covers apparel, accessories, and golf culture for DORMIED. He has been tracking brand drops and collaborations since 2018.

Arnie McNair sells a deliberately small collection: two polos, one short, one cashmere sweater, two visors, a belt and a wallet. The Arnie Polo is the flagship at $105 and is currently sold out in every colour and size. Everything is made in seasonal small batches from natural fibres, almost entirely in the United States, and the brand does not restock. If you have found this page because you searched the brand and hit a store where nothing is available, that is not a glitch. It is the operating model.

In one sentence: Arnie McNair's collection consists of the Arnie Polo and McNair Polo, Washington Shorts, the Vincenzo Cashmere sweater, the AM and George visors, and the Eisenhower belt and wallet, all produced in limited seasonal runs from natural fibres.

Eight products across five categories. That is the entire catalogue, and the restraint is intentional rather than a sign of a young brand still building out a range.

The polos

The Arnie Polo is the flagship and the piece most people are searching for. It sells at $105 in Stone, Sage Green and Butter Yellow, sized S through 2XL. At the time of writing every colour and every size shows as sold out, which is the normal end-state of an Arnie McNair release rather than an unusual event.

The McNair Polo is the three-button spread-collar version, a slightly more formal cut for the same natural-fibre approach.

Both sit at a price point worth putting in context. $105 is above the mass market and below the luxury houses. It is roughly what Malbon charges and more than Pins & Aces will allow anything on its site to cost. What you are paying for is American manufacturing and natural fibre rather than a logo, which is a harder sell and a narrower market by design.

Shorts, outerwear and accessories

Washington Shorts cover the bottom half of the range. The Vincenzo Cashmere sweater is the outerwear piece and the one place the brand openly breaks its American-manufacturing rule, sourcing cashmere from Italy and labelling it plainly rather than burying it.

The accessories are where the brand's character shows most clearly. There are two visors, the AM Tour Visor and the George Visor, plus an Eisenhower Belt and an Eisenhower Cash Cover in the leather goods category.

The naming is not incidental. Washington, Eisenhower, George: the catalogue reads like a roll call of American history, which is consistent with a brand built explicitly around American manufacturing and traditional golf dress. Eisenhower in particular is a deliberate choice for a golf brand, given his association with Augusta National and his standing as the president who made golf a mainstream American pursuit.

The AM initials carry different weight. Pieces marked AM are dedicated to the founder's brother, a United States Marine who died in 2004, with proceeds going to Stop Soldier Suicide. It is the one part of the catalogue that is not really about clothing.

Why everything is sold out

Arnie McNair produces in limited seasonal runs and does not restock. The brand's own description of the model is small-batch production that keeps it close to the process, making fewer garments more deliberately. Subscribers to the mailing list get notice of each release, which is functionally the only way to buy at launch.

That creates the pattern anyone searching the brand runs into: high interest, low availability, and a store that frequently has nothing to sell. Shipping is estimated at seven to ten days once an order is placed, and the brand ships to around 28 countries.

The scarcity is real rather than manufactured. It falls out of how the clothes are actually made, which is a different thing from a hype drop engineered to sell out in ninety seconds.

Where the brand sits

DORMIED tracks search interest across 215 golf brands in 10 markets. Arnie McNair currently ranks 119th, up four places month over month with a DI score of 1.5 and roughly 3,600 monthly searches.

That is a modest absolute position, and it is worth being straight about it: this is a small brand. What makes it notable is the trajectory. In April its three-month change was up 371 percent, and in May it was up 205 percent, which is among the steepest sustained climbs in the Index from a standing start. The brand launched in January 2026. Everything it has, it has built in eight months.

The current picture is more mixed than those figures suggest. The three-month change has now turned negative at -13.7 percent, which is what happens to a made-to-order brand between seasonal releases. Interest spikes when a drop opens and cools while the mailing list waits for the next one. The monthly figure, up 24.1 percent, is the more useful read.

FAQ

What does Arnie McNair sell?

Two polos (the Arnie Polo and the McNair Polo), Washington Shorts, the Vincenzo Cashmere sweater, two visors (AM Tour and George), and Eisenhower leather goods comprising a belt and a cash cover.

How much is the Arnie McNair polo?

The Arnie Polo is $105. It comes in Stone, Sage Green and Butter Yellow, in sizes S through 2XL.

Why is Arnie McNair always sold out?

The brand produces in limited seasonal batches and does not restock. Items sell through and stay sold out until the next release. Joining the mailing list is the only reliable way to know when a drop is coming.

Where is Arnie McNair clothing made?

Almost entirely in the United States. The brand prioritises American manufacturing and American-grown natural fibres, with Italian cashmere as the labelled exception where the craftsmanship is not available domestically.

What does the AM on Arnie McNair products mean?

Pieces marked AM are dedicated to the founder's brother, a United States Marine who died in 2004. Proceeds from those items go to Stop Soldier Suicide.

Does Arnie McNair ship internationally?

Yes, to roughly 28 countries including Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, Japan, South Korea and most of the European Union. Estimated shipping is seven to ten days.

What fabrics does Arnie McNair use?

Natural fibres only, chosen for breathability and longevity rather than synthetic performance materials. The brand's stated position is that natural fibres improve with wear and avoid the compromises of synthetics.

Is Arnie McNair worth it?

That depends on what you are buying. At $105 a polo you are paying for American manufacturing, natural fibre and small-batch production rather than a recognisable logo. By DORMIED's tracking the brand ranks 119th of 215 with rising monthly interest, so the market is finding it, but it remains a small and deliberately limited operation.
