Antoine Rozner is a French professional on the DP World Tour with three wins on that circuit, including the 2020 Golf in Dubai Championship and the 2021 Commercial Bank Qatar Masters. He turned pro in 2014 and has represented France in amateur and professional team competition. The bag reads full Titleist through the long game: a TSi3 driver at 10 degrees with a HZRDUS Smoke Black Prototype 62 shaft, a U-500 utility at the 2, a 620 CB at the 3, and 620 MB blades from 4 through 9, all on Dynamic Gold Tour Issue X100. The putter is an Odyssey Stroke Lab Ten S, mallet shape, and the ball is a Pro V1x. Compared to the 2021 snapshot, the framework holds: same driver model, same iron stack of U-500 into 620 CB into 620 MB, same Ten-shape Odyssey mallet, same ball.
