Tour Equipment

Chez Reavie

🇺🇸#666·UPDATED Sep 22, 2026

Quick Answer

Chez Reavie plays a TaylorMade Qi4D driver, TaylorMade P7CB irons, Titleist Vokey Design SM11 wedges, a Scotty Cameron Phantom 9.2A Prototype putter, and the Titleist Pro V1x ball, as recorded in the September 2026 bag snapshot. The driver is built with a Fujikura Ventus Blue 6 X shaft.

Chez Reavie turned professional in 2004 after taking the 2001 NCAA individual title at Arizona State. He has two PGA Tour wins, the 2008 RBC Canadian Open and the 2019 Travelers Championship, plus a tie for third at the 2019 U.S. Open. Position off the tee, not speed, has carried the bulk of his scoring. The current driver is a Qi4D at 9 degrees with a Fujikura Ventus Blue 6 X, and the irons are back inside TaylorMade, a P7CB set from 5-PW on KBS Tour C-Taper 120 S. Across the three snapshots the driver reads TaylorMade, then Titleist TSR2, then TaylorMade again, and the irons trace the same loop through New Level in between. The putter is the one slot that never repeats a brand: Odyssey O-Works #7, a PING PLD Prototype, now a Scotty Cameron Phantom 9.2A Prototype. The ball stays Titleist throughout, Pro V1x at both ends with Pro V1 in the middle.

Bag History

3 snapshots tracked, 2020-2026

The 2020 bag was TaylorMade through the long game, a SIM Max and an M2 driver with P750 irons, paired with an Odyssey O-Works #7 and a Pro V1x. By the 2023 snapshot the driver had moved to a Titleist TSR2, the irons to New Level 623-CB, the putter to a PING PLD Prototype and the ball to Pro V1. The current setup returns to TaylorMade at both ends of the bag with the Qi4D and P7CB irons, while the putter moves again, this time to a Scotty Cameron Phantom 9.2A Prototype. Titleist golf balls are the only constant across all three snapshots.
