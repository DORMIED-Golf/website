Quick Answer

Adam Long plays a TaylorMade Stealth driver, TaylorMade P790 irons, TaylorMade MG3 wedges, and a Scotty Cameron Phantom T-5 Proto putter, as recorded in the February 2022 bag snapshot. The driver is built with a True Temper Project X HZRDUS Small Batch Smoke Green 60 6.5 X shaft.

Adam Long is an American professional on the PGA Tour, best known for winning the 2019 Desert Classic as a rookie, holding off Phil Mickelson and Adam Hadwin down the stretch at PGA West. That victory secured his Tour card and remains the centerpiece of his résumé, with a handful of close calls and a runner-up at the 2021 Honda Classic filling in the years since. The bag is a TaylorMade build through and through, anchored by the Stealth driver at 9 degrees with a Project X HZRDUS Smoke Green 60 6.5 X shaft. The iron setup splits the difference between distance and feel, a P790 4-iron leading into P760s from 5 through pitching wedge, all on Project X 6.5 X. Compared to the 2020 snapshot, the driver moved from SIM Max to Stealth and the Scotty Cameron Futura X5R Tour gave way to a Phantom T-5 Proto, while the P790/P760 iron combo carried over unchanged.

Bag History

2 snapshots tracked, 2020-2022

Across both snapshots, TaylorMade has been the sole driver and iron supplier, with the P790 long iron and P760 mid-to-short iron pairing held over from 2020 into the current build. The driver progressed from SIM Max to Stealth, and the putter moved within the Scotty Cameron lineup from a Futura X5R Tour to a Phantom T-5 Proto. The throughline is a TaylorMade-and-Scotty setup that has been refreshed at the head, not rebuilt.
