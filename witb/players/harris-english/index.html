Quick Answer

Harris English plays a PING G430 LST driver, PING G410 Crossover irons, PING Glide 4.0 wedges, a PING Scottsdale Hohum putter, and the Titleist Pro V1 ball, as recorded in the January 2025 bag snapshot. The driver is built with a Fujikura Ventus TR Blue 6 X shaft.

Harris English is a PGA Tour veteran out of the University of Georgia with multiple Tour wins, a Presidents Cup and Ryder Cup appearance on his ledger, and a runner-up at the 2021 U.S. Open. His 2021 season included victories at the Sentry Tournament of Champions and the Travelers Championship, the latter requiring eight playoff holes against Kramer Hickok. The bag is a PING clinic from tee to green. The G430 LST sits up top with a Ventus TR Blue, replacing the G400 that anchored the 2021 snapshots, and the Blueprint T irons (4-9) now follow the G410 Crossover at the 3, an update from the standard Blueprint heads carried through 2022. The Scottsdale Hohum putter has not moved across any snapshot on file, and the Pro V1 has been the ball of record throughout. Across five snapshots, every club in the bag carries a PING headcover.

Bag History

5 snapshots tracked, 2021-2025

Across all five snapshots from January 2021 forward, English has played PING from driver through irons, with the Scottsdale Hohum putter and Titleist Pro V1 ball as constants. The driver moved from the G400 in the 2021 snapshots to the G430 LST by the end of 2022 and has held through 2025. The iron set updated from PING Blueprint to Blueprint T between the 2022 and 2024 snapshots, while the G410 Crossover 3 has stayed in the bag throughout.
