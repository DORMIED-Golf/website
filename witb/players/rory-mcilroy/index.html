Quick Answer

Rory McIlroy plays a TaylorMade Qi4D driver, TaylorMade P760 irons, TaylorMade MG5 wedges, a TaylorMade Spider Tour X putter, and the TaylorMade TP5 ball, as recorded in the April 2026 bag snapshot. The driver is built with a Fujikura Ventus Black 6 X shaft.

Rory McIlroy is a Northern Irish professional on the PGA Tour with five major championships to his name, including the 2025 Masters that completed the career Grand Slam. He has held the world number one ranking across multiple stints since first reaching it in 2012 and has compiled more than two dozen PGA Tour victories alongside Ryder Cup appearances dating back to 2010. The current build runs a TaylorMade Qi4D at nine degrees with a Fujikura Ventus Black 6 X, paired with a P760 4-iron and Rors Proto blades from five through nine on Project X 7.0 shafts. Every snapshot in the dataset, going back to early 2021, shows a full TaylorMade setup across driver, irons, putter, and ball. The Rors Proto irons have been a fixture throughout, and the Spider platform has carried the putter slot since the TP Juno was set aside after the March 2021 snapshot.

Bag History

16 snapshots tracked, 2021-2026

Across sixteen snapshots from March 2021 to April 2026, the bag has been entirely TaylorMade at every check. Drivers move from SIM2 to Stealth Plus to Stealth 2 Plus, then through Qi10 LS, Qi10, and into the current Qi4D. Iron setups cycle between P7MB, P770, P760, and unspecified protos around the constant Rors Proto blades, while the putter shifts from the TP Juno to the Spider X Hydro Blast, then Spider Tour X3, and settles on the Spider Tour X. The ball was TP5x through early 2024 before TP5 took over in the more recent snapshots.
