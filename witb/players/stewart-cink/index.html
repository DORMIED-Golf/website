Tour Equipment

Stewart Cink

🇺🇸#2169·UPDATED Aug 11, 2026

Quick Answer

Stewart Cink plays a PING G430 Max 10K driver, PING i210 irons, PING Glide Forged wedges, a PING Vault 2.0 Ketsch Stealth putter, and the Titleist Pro V1x ball, as recorded in the June 2024 bag snapshot. The driver is built with a Graphite Design Tour AD XC 6 X shaft.

Stewart Cink is a veteran American professional who turned pro in 1995 and has spent his career on the PGA Tour. He won the 2009 Open Championship at Turnberry in a playoff over Tom Watson, and his résumé includes eight PGA Tour titles along with multiple Ryder Cup and Presidents Cup appearances. After a quiet stretch, he returned to the winner's circle at the 2020 Safeway Open and the 2021 RBC Heritage. The current setup is a full PING build from tee to green, anchored by the G430 Max 10K driver bent to 9.5 degrees with a Graphite Design Tour AD XC shaft. The iron stack is a three-model mix, i210 long irons into Blueprint T mid and short irons, with an i230 utility wedge closing the set. The Vault 2.0 Ketsch Stealth putter has been in the bag across every recorded snapshot, paired throughout with a Titleist Pro V1x.

Bag History

4 snapshots tracked, 2021-2024

Across four snapshots dating to 2021, Cink has stayed entirely within PING for driver and irons. The driver progressed from the G425 Max in 2021 and 2022 to the G430 Max in 2023 and the G430 Max 10K in the current build. Iron models have shifted between i200, i210, i230, and Blueprint T, but the brand has not. The Vault 2.0 Ketsch Stealth putter appears in three of four snapshots, with a PLD White noted in 2022, and the Pro V1x ball is constant throughout.
