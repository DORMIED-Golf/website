Kevin Kisner

🇺🇸#1003·UPDATED Jul 21, 2026

Kevin Kisner is a four-time PGA Tour winner whose résumé includes the 2021 Wyndham Championship and a runner-up finish at the 2018 Open Championship at Carnoustie, where he lost a four-man playoff. He reached the final of the WGC-Dell Technologies Match Play in back-to-back years, winning the title in 2019. Since stepping back from a full schedule, he has moved into a Golf Channel and NBC booth role while still teeing it up selectively. The current setup pairs two Callaway nine-degree heads, a Paradym Ai Smoke Triple Diamond Plus and an Elyte Triple Diamond, both built with Mitsubishi Diamana PD 60 TX shafts. The irons flipped from Wilson Staff Model CB and Utility blades to a Srixon ZXi5 and ZXi7 combo on Dynamic Gold Mid 115 S300s, and the L.A.B. DF3 has given way to an Odyssey Ai-ONE Square 2 Square Jailbird. Driver has stayed Callaway across every snapshot, and the Pro V1 remains the ball of choice.

Bag History

6 snapshots tracked, 2021-2025

Across six snapshots, Callaway has been the only driver brand in the bag, moving from the GBB and Great Big Bertha builds through the Paradym Ai Smoke Triple Diamond Max and into the current Paradym Ai Smoke and Elyte Triple Diamond pair. The iron rotation went from Callaway Apex Pro and Apex UT through 2022, to Wilson Staff Model CB and Utility through 2024, then to a Srixon ZXi5 and ZXi7 combo in the current bag. Putter has cycled through several Odyssey models, a brief L.A.B. DF3 stint in 2024, and back to Odyssey with the Ai-ONE Jailbird. The Titleist Pro V1 has been the constant ball, with one Pro V1x snapshot in 2021.
