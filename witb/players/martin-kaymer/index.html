Martin Kaymer

🇩🇪#779·UPDATED Jul 21, 2026

Martin Kaymer is a German professional who turned pro in 2005 and reached world number one in February 2011. His major wins came at the 2010 PGA Championship at Whistling Straits and the 2014 U.S. Open at Pinehurst No. 2, where he led wire to wire. He also holed the clinching putt for Europe at the 2012 Ryder Cup at Medinah and won The Players Championship the same year as his second major. The current setup is a TaylorMade rack from tee to green minus the putter, fronted by a Stealth 2 Plus driver at 9 degrees on an Accra TZ6 Proto 65 M5 shaft. The irons are a full set of P7TW blades, 3 through 9, on Dynamic Gold AMT Tour White Tour Issue X100 shafts, a Tiger Woods signature profile Kaymer was already using in the 2020 snapshot. The putter is a PING PLD Prototype, the lone non-TaylorMade head in the bag.

Bag History

2 snapshots tracked, 2020-2023

Across both snapshots, the driver head has stayed inside the TaylorMade line, moving from a SIM in 2020 to a Stealth 2 Plus in the current bag. The P7TW blades carry through both setups, with a P790 UDI filling the long-iron slot in 2020 and a 3-iron P7TW handling that role now. The putter is the only category to leave TaylorMade, shifting from a Truss TM1 to a PING PLD Prototype.
