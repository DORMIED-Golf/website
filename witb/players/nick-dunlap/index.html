Nick Dunlap

🇺🇸#401·UPDATED Aug 11, 2026

Quick Answer

Nick Dunlap plays a PING G430 LST driver, Srixon ZXiU irons, TaylorMade MG3 wedges, an Odyssey O-Works #7 Tank putter, and the Titleist Pro V1 ball, as recorded in the March 2025 bag snapshot. The driver is built with a True Temper Project X HZRDUS 60 TX shaft.

Nick Dunlap is the Alabama product who became the first amateur to win on the PGA Tour since 1991, taking The American Express in January 2024 before turning professional days later. He added a second Tour title at the Barracuda Championship that summer, joining a short list of players to win as an amateur and a pro in the same season. The driver is the headline movement in the bag: a PING G430 LST with a Project X HZRDUS 60 TX has replaced the TaylorMade Qi10 LS he won with as an amateur, a change visible by the September 2024 snapshot and carried into 2025. Everything around it has held. The TaylorMade P7MC blades (4-9), Odyssey O-Works #7 Tank, and Titleist Pro V1 have been fixtures across every snapshot on file, and the Srixon utility iron at the top of the set has remained a Srixon build, currently the ZXiU with a Modus3 GOST X.
