Tour Equipment

Shane Lowry

🇮🇪#45·UPDATED Jun 30, 2026

Shane Lowry is an Irish professional whose career breakthrough came as an amateur at the 2009 Irish Open, with the defining result arriving a decade later when he won the 2019 Open Championship at Royal Portrush by six strokes. He has carried multiple Ryder Cup appearances for Europe and competes across the PGA Tour and DP World Tour. The bag is a Srixon build from tee through approach, with the ZXi LS driver running a Mitsubishi Diamana D+ White 70 TX, a ZXiU 3-iron utility on Ventus VeloCore+ Blue, and a ZXi5/ZXi7 combo set on KBS Tour 130 X shafts. Every driver snapshot on file traces back to Srixon, moving from the Z 585 in 2020 through the ZX5 family and the ZXi before settling on the ZXi LS. The non-Srixon constants are the TaylorMade Spider Tour Z putter, in play since the 2025 snapshots, and the Z-Star XV ball across every entry.

Bag History

8 snapshots tracked, 2020-2026

Across eight snapshots from 2020 forward, every driver and iron set belongs to Srixon, moving Z 585 to ZX5 to ZX5 MKII to ZXi to the current ZXi LS, with the iron lineage shifting from Z 585/785 to ZX5/ZX7 to the present ZXi5/ZXi7 combo. The utility slot has tracked the same generational path from Z U85 to ZX U to ZX U MKII to ZXiU. The putter ran as an Odyssey 2-Ball through 2024, beginning with the Stroke Lab Exo and moving to the White Hot OG, before the TaylorMade Spider Tour Z took over by the May 2025 snapshot. The Srixon Z-Star XV ball is present in every entry.
