Max Homa

🇺🇸#120·UPDATED Jun 2, 2026

Max Homa is a six-time PGA Tour winner and a Cal Berkeley product who took the 2013 NCAA individual title before turning pro. He represented the United States at the 2022 Presidents Cup and the 2023 Ryder Cup, with the bulk of his Tour wins coming in a four-season stretch from 2021 through 2023. The Scotty Cameron T5.5 Proto has been in the bag across every snapshot on file, dating back to a Phantom X T5.5 prototype variant in 2021, and the Pro V1 has not moved either. The rest of the bag tells a different story. Driver and irons sat under the Titleist umbrella from 2021 through August 2024, running TSi3 into TSR3 into GT3, paired with T100s and 620 MB blades. The 2025 snapshots show a full move to Cobra up front: DS-ADAPT LS to start the year, DS-ADAPT X by September, with a 3D printed long iron, King CB, and King MB blades behind it.

Bag History

9 snapshots tracked, 2021-2025

From 2021 through August 2024, Homa played Titleist drivers (TSi3, TSR3, GT3) and Titleist irons anchored by the 620 MB, with T100 and T100s filling the longer slots. The January 2025 snapshot shows Cobra in the driver and iron slots for the first time in the dataset, with the DS-ADAPT LS and LIMIT3D alongside King CB and MB blades. By September 2025 the driver has moved to the DS-ADAPT X and a Cobra 3D Printed Tour long iron replaces the LIMIT3D. The Scotty Cameron T5.5 Proto putter and the Pro V1 ball are constants across all nine snapshots.
