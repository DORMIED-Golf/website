Cameron Davis is an Australian professional on the PGA Tour with two wins at the Rocket Mortgage Classic, taking the title in 2021 and again in 2024. He turned professional in 2017 after capturing the Australian Open as an amateur-era closer, and has represented Australia at the Olympics. The current bag is a full Titleist build, anchored by a GT3 driver at 9 degrees in the D4 SureFit setting with a Fujikura Ventus TR Black 7 X. The 620 CB irons from 4 through pitching wedge have been a fixture since the 2021 snapshot, paired now with a U505 driving iron in the 3 slot after earlier runs through the U-500 and T100 utility long irons. Driver has stayed Titleist across every snapshot on record, moving TSi3 to TSR3 to GT3. The Pro V1 has been in play since 2022, after a Pro V1x run in 2021. Putter remains a Scotty Cameron, currently a Newport Tour Prototype.

Bag History

5 snapshots tracked, 2021-2025

Across five snapshots from 2021 to 2025, the bag has stayed entirely Titleist top to bottom. Driver moved TSi3 to TSR3 to GT3, with the 620 CB blades holding their spot since 2022 after a 620 MB run in 2021. Long iron duties have rotated through U-500, T100, and now the U505, and the ball switched from Pro V1x in 2021 to Pro V1 from 2022 onward. Putter has cycled through several Scotty Cameron models, currently a Newport Tour Prototype.
