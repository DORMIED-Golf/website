Jon Rahm

OWGR #11 · updated May 26, 2026

Jon Rahm is a Spanish professional golfer, a former world number one, and a two-time major champion, having won the 2021 U.S. Open at Torrey Pines and the 2023 Masters Tournament. He moved to LIV Golf in December 2023 and currently sits at OWGR #11. The Odyssey Rossie S head has been a constant through every Callaway-era snapshot, first as a White Hot OG and now in Ai-ONE dress. The Apex TCB blades in the 5-iron through pitching wedge slot have been in the bag since the earliest 2021 record, with an Apex Pro 4-iron and an Apex UT 21-degree driving iron rounding out the top of the set. The driver line has cycled through the Triple Diamond family on schedule, Epic Speed to Rogue ST to Paradym to Paradym Ai Smoke and now the Elyte, all sleeved with a Fujikura Ventus Black 7 X. The Chrome Soft X gave way to the Chrome Tour after the 2023 ball relaunch.

Bag History

10 snapshots tracked, 2019-2025

Rahm's late-2019 and 2020 snapshots show a full TaylorMade setup, SIM driver, P750 irons, Spider X, and TP5. The record then jumps to March 2021 with the bag already fully Callaway and Odyssey, anchored by the Apex TCB irons, an X Forged UT, and a 2-Ball Ten prototype putter. The Rossie S took over later in 2021 and has stayed in the bag since, while the driver progressed through Epic Speed, Rogue ST, Paradym, Paradym Ai Smoke, and now the Elyte Triple Diamond. The current build adds an Apex Pro 4-iron and the Apex UT at 21 degrees, with the Chrome Tour replacing the long-running Chrome Soft X.
