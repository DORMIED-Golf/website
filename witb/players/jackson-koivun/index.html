Quick Answer

Jackson Koivun plays a Titleist GT2 driver, Titleist T250 irons, Titleist Vokey Design SM11 wedges, a Scotty Cameron Phantom 9.5R Tour Prototype putter, and the Titleist Pro V1 ball, as recorded in the July 2026 bag snapshot. The driver is built with a Fujikura Speeder 661 TR X shaft.

Jackson Koivun arrived at Auburn as a decorated junior and left his freshman and sophomore seasons with the Fred Haskins Award and the Ben Hogan Award, the two headline honors in men's college golf. He has been the frontrunner in the PGA Tour University Accelerated race, the pathway that converts college performance directly into Tour status, and has represented the United States in Walker Cup competition. The bag is Titleist top to bottom, with a GT2 driver at 8 degrees on a Fujikura Speeder 661 TR X, a build that leans lower-spin and stout rather than max-launch. The iron setup is the tell: a T250 utility 3-iron on Tour AD DI HY 95 X, a T100 4-iron on Project X 6.5, and 620 MB Prototype blades from 5 through 9 on the same Project X 6.5 shafts. A Scotty Cameron Phantom 9.5R Tour Prototype and a Pro V1 close it out.
