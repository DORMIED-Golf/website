Quim Vidal

Quick Answer

Quim Vidal plays a Ping G440 LST driver, Ping iDi irons, Ping S259 Midnight wedges, a L.A.B. Golf VZN.1i putter, and the Titleist Pro V1x ball, as recorded in the July 2026 bag snapshot. The driver is built with a Fujikura Ventus Red TR 6 X shaft.

Quim Vidal is a Spanish professional out of Barcelona who turned pro in 2021 after an amateur career that included European Amateur appearances and a stint in the college ranks in the United States. He has built his early professional resume across the Alps Tour and Challenge Tour, chasing status through the developmental circuits that feed the DP World Tour. The current setup leans Ping from tee through the irons, with a G440 LST driver at 9 degrees on a Fujikura Ventus Red TR 6 X, a Ping iDi 3-hybrid slotted in on a Project X HZRDUS 90g Hybrid shaft, and a Ping i230 iron set running 4 through 9 on Project X Rifle 6.0 steel. The wedges and short game specifics beyond the L.A.B. Golf VZN.1i putter are not on record here. Ball is a Titleist Pro V1x. The Ping commitment through the long clubs and irons is the through-line of the build.
