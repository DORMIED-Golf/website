Abraham Ancer

🇲🇽#297·UPDATED May 28, 2026

Abraham Ancer is a Mexican professional who came up through the Web.com Tour, broke through on the PGA Tour with a win at the 2021 WGC-FedEx St. Jude Invitational, and represented the International side at the 2019 and 2021 Presidents Cups before leaving for LIV Golf in 2022, where he anchors the Fireballs GC roster. The Paradym Triple Diamond at 9 degrees, bent to 10, runs a Mitsubishi Tensei CK Pro Blue 60 TX, continuing a Callaway-only driver run across every snapshot on file. The Miura TC-201 blades have been a fixture since 2021, now built on MMT Taper Iron 105 TX shafts, and the Pro V1x has never moved. The notable wrinkle is the bag carrying two Odyssey putters, a White Hot No. 5 alongside a Tri-Hot 5K Double Wide, the latest iteration of a No. 5 shape Ancer has leaned on for years.

Bag History

4 snapshots tracked, 2021-2023

Across four snapshots from April 2021 to June 2023, Callaway has been the only driver brand in the bag, moving from the Epic Speed Triple Diamond LS through a brief Rogue ST Triple Diamond LS pairing in early 2022 and into the current Paradym Triple Diamond. The Miura TC-201 irons and Titleist Pro V1x ball are present in every snapshot. The Odyssey putter line has stayed constant in family, with the White Hot No. 5 Stroke Lab giving way to a White Hot No. 5 and a Tri-Hot 5K Double Wide now sharing the bag.
