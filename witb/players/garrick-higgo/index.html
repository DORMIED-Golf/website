Quick Answer

Garrick Higgo plays a Titleist GT2 driver, Titleist T200 irons, Titleist Vokey Design SM10 wedges, a L.A.B. Golf OZ.1 Pro putter, and the Titleist Pro V1 ball, as recorded in the April 2025 bag snapshot. The driver is built with a Graphite Design Tour AD DI 7 X shaft.

Garrick Higgo is a South African professional who came up through the Sunshine Tour and the DP World Tour, where he won three times in his first full season in 2021 and earned a PGA Tour card off the back of it. He took his maiden PGA Tour title at the Palmetto Championship that same year as a rookie and added a second at the 2024 Barracuda Championship. The current setup leads with a Titleist GT2 driver at 11 degrees, a Graphite Design Tour AD DI 7 X in the hosel, and a T200 3-iron fronting a T100 4-PW block, all on Project X 6.5. The constants across every snapshot on file are Titleist irons (T100 has been in the bag at every check-in) and a Titleist ball. The driver line has moved through TSi3, TSR4, a brief TaylorMade BRNR Mini pairing in early 2024, and back to a Titleist GT2, while the putter has settled into L.A.B. Golf after years of Scotty Cameron.

Bag History

5 snapshots tracked, 2021-2025

Across five snapshots from 2021 to 2025, Titleist irons and a Titleist ball have never left the bag, with the T100 present every time. The driver progression runs Titleist TSi3 in 2021 and 2022, Titleist TSR4 in 2023, a TSR4 plus TaylorMade BRNR Mini pairing in February 2024, and a Titleist GT2 by April 2025. The putter arc moves from a Scotty Cameron Phantom X 5.5 to a Circle T 5.5 Proto, then a T5.5, before shifting to L.A.B. Golf with the DF3 in 2024 and the OZ.1 Pro in the current bag.
