Tour Equipment

Joel Dahmen

🇺🇸#209·UPDATED Jun 23, 2026

Joel Dahmen is a PGA Tour pro from Arizona who broke through at the 2021 Corales Puntacana Championship for his first Tour win. He has played his way into multiple major championships, including a T10 at the 2022 U.S. Open at The Country Club, and built a following well beyond his ranking through the Netflix Full Swing series. The driver head is a PING G440 LST at 9 degrees with a Diamana TB 60 TX, the latest step in a PING run that stretches from the G425 LST through the G430 LST across every snapshot on file. The iron setup pairs a PXG 0317 CB 4-iron with 0211 ST from 5 through pitching wedge, all on Dynamic Gold Tour Issue X100. PXG irons and the Titleist Pro V1x have anchored the bag since the Jailbird era began on the green, with the current Odyssey Ai-ONE Square 2 Square Jailbird the newest version of a mallet he has trusted for years.

Bag History

6 snapshots tracked, 2021-2026

Across six snapshots the driver stays PING throughout, moving G425 LST to G430 LST to the current G440 LST. The irons shift from a PXG 0311T Gen2 set in 2021 into the 0211 ST family, with a 0317 ST long iron added by late 2023 and the current build mixing a 0317 CB 4-iron with 0211 ST mid and short irons. The putter path runs from a Scotty Cameron Roundback Prototype through two Odyssey White Hot OG models into the Jailbird family, where it has stayed. The ball is Titleist Pro V1 early, then Pro V1x from 2023 forward.
