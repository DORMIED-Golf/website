Kevin Na

🇺🇸#1123·UPDATED Jul 28, 2026

Kevin Na is a five-time PGA Tour winner whose victories include the Justin Timberlake Shriners Hospitals for Children Open, the Greenbrier Classic, and the 2021 Sony Open in Hawaii. Born in South Korea and raised in California, he turned professional out of high school in 2001 and built a career known for elite short game and one of the more deliberate pre-shot routines on Tour. The bag is Callaway from tee through approach. The current driver is the Epic Speed Triple Diamond at 9 degrees with a Graphite Design Tour AD GP 6 TX, an update from the GBB Epic logged in the January 2021 snapshot. The iron setup has held: a Rogue Pro 4-iron paired with Apex Pro 16s from the 5 through pitching wedge, all on Dynamic Gold Tour Issue S400. The Odyssey Toulon Madison putter and Pro V1x ball carry over from the previous snapshot unchanged.
