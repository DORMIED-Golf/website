J.T. Poston

🇺🇸#32·UPDATED Jun 30, 2026

J.T. Poston is a PGA Tour winner out of Western Carolina, with victories at the 2019 Wyndham Championship and the 2022 John Deere Classic, the latter closing bogey-free across 72 holes. He has represented the United States at the Presidents Cup and built a reputation as one of the tour's steadier ball-strikers and short-game operators. The bag reads Titleist top to bottom on the long clubs, with a GTS3 at 9 degrees on a Diamana BF 60 TX up front and a T150 driving iron leading into a T100 set from 5 through 9, all on Project X 6.5. Across every snapshot on file the driver has carried a Titleist badge, moving TS3 to TSR3 to GT3 and now GTS3. The larger change sits on the green, where the Scotty Cameron GoLo 5 Black Tour Prototype that anchored four straight snapshots has been replaced by a TaylorMade Spider Tour X.

Bag History

5 snapshots tracked, 2020-2026
