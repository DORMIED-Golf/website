Joaquin Niemann

🇨🇱#54·UPDATED Jun 30, 2026

Joaquin Niemann turned professional in 2018 after a decorated amateur career that included a stint at world number one in the amateur ranking. The Chilean has won twice on the PGA Tour, represented the International side at the Presidents Cup, and joined LIV Golf in 2023, where he has added multiple individual victories. PING runs the bag from tee to green. The G430 LST sits in the driver slot with a Graphite Design Tour AD DI 6 X, the Blueprint S blades cover 5 through pitching wedge on Project X 6.5, and the PLD Anser Prototype putter has not moved across any of the five snapshots on file. The notable change in the irons came between 2023 and 2024, when Niemann moved out of the iBlade and into the Blueprint S. The driver progression tracks PING throughout, stepping from the G410 LST through the G430 LST. Ball of choice remains the Titleist Pro V1x.

Bag History

5 snapshots tracked, 2021-2025

Across five snapshots dating to 2021, Niemann has stayed entirely in PING for driver, irons, and putter. The PLD Anser Prototype putter is the constant, present in every bag on record. The driver moved from the G410 LST in the 2021 and 2022 snapshots to the G430 LST by 2023 and forward, and the irons shifted from the iBlade to the Blueprint S between the 2023 and 2024 snapshots, with the Titleist Pro V1x logged as the ball from 2022 on.
