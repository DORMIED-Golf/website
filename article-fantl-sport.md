# What Is Fantl Sport? The New Zealand Brand Built on Still Being Out There Tomorrow

*By Adam R. | Last updated: September 8, 2026*

*Adam R. covers apparel, accessories, and golf culture for DORMIED. He has been tracking brand drops and collaborations since 2018.*

Fantl Sport is a golf apparel brand founded in New Zealand in 2021, built on a design position that runs against how most golf clothing is sold. The brand's line is "Tools to keep you on course", and the promise underneath it is longevity rather than novelty: considered golf utility that balances function and refined design, built, in the brand's words, to help people play and keep playing for as long as possible. No logos shouting across the chest, no seasonal gimmicks. A small range of pieces designed to work on a course and then keep working everywhere else.

In one sentence: Fantl Sport is a New Zealand technical golf apparel brand founded in 2021, known for minimalist design, premium pricing, and a deliberately restrained range built around versatility and longevity rather than volume.

## A golf brand from the wrong country

The golf apparel industry is overwhelmingly American and British. That is where the money is, where the tours are, and where nearly every brand DORMIED tracks is headquartered. New Zealand is not on that map.

That is part of what makes Fantl interesting. It has built an international following from a country of five million people, without a domestic market big enough to sustain it and without the industry infrastructure that surrounds a brand launching in Los Angeles or London. New Zealand retailers who stock it have made the point directly: the golf industry tends to be overrun by brands from the USA and UK, and New Zealanders are creating things that deserve more credit.

The brand does not lean on its origin as a marketing hook, which is notable in itself. There is no kiwi iconography, no All Blacks borrowing, none of the national shorthand a smaller-market brand usually reaches for. The clothes are the argument. What the brand does claim about its home is narrower and more useful: golf utility proven in New Zealand, built for the world.

## Built to last, which is a harder sell than it sounds

Fantl's design position is unusually explicit for an apparel company, and it has sharpened over time. The brand now frames the whole proposition around durability of use rather than newness: "It's not about how you play today; it's about still being out there tomorrow."

In practice that means a tight range. Polos, pants, hoodies and outerwear in restrained colourways, cut to work off the course as easily as on it. The names carry a bit of dry humour, the Masters Monday Polo and the Peter Pant among them, but the pieces themselves are quiet. Comfort without distraction, protection without restriction, and versatility that extends past the 18th green.

The pricing is not quiet. Fantl sits at the premium end, noticeably above better-known brands, and reviewers have said so plainly while making the case that the quality justifies it. For a young brand from a small market with no tour stable to trade on, holding a premium price is a real bet: it removes the volume play entirely and stakes everything on people finding the product good enough to pay for.

There is a sustainability thread running through the operation too, including biodegradable packaging, which fits the wider philosophy. A brand telling you to buy things that last has to mean it.

## The advertising is the interesting part

Most small apparel brands market through Instagram grids and influencer seeding. Fantl made a film.

In May 2025 the brand released a campaign called One Shot at Life, directed by Reagan Butler through the production company Supernormal. It was covered by The Drum, a marketing industry publication that does not typically write about small golf labels, and the piece framed it as a poetic ode to golf and resilience, built around not being dismayed when things do not go your way. The film is shot in black and white and told from the perspective of the founder's grandfather, with golf standing in for the business of getting knocked down and going again.

That is a genuinely unusual move. A brand at Fantl's scale spending on a directed film with a real production company, and pitching it as an idea rather than a product, is behaving like a much larger company. It also tells you what the brand thinks it is selling, which is not moisture-wicking fabric.

## Why it matters

DORMIED covers a lot of golf brands built on volume, on drops, on hype cycles and on saying more. Fantl is doing close to the opposite, from a country nobody watches for golf apparel, at prices that leave no room for error.

The bet is that a small number of well-made, unbranded, genuinely versatile pieces will out-earn a big catalogue of trend-chasing ones. That is a harder business than it sounds, and it is why most brands do not try it. Whether it scales is an open question. Whether it is interesting is not.

## FAQ

**What is Fantl Sport?**
Fantl Sport is a New Zealand golf apparel brand making technical, minimalist clothing designed to work on the course and in everyday life. The brand's current line is "Tools to keep you on course".

**When was Fantl Sport founded?**
2021, in New Zealand.

**Who founded Fantl Sport?**
Dan Fantl is listed as the founder on his own professional profile and in podcast appearances, including the Fairgame podcast. The brand's own site does not name a founder, so treat this as publicly stated rather than company-confirmed.

**Where is Fantl Sport based?**
New Zealand, which makes it an outlier in a golf apparel industry dominated by American and British brands.

**What does Fantl Sport sell?**
Polos, pants, hoodies and outerwear in a deliberately small range, including pieces such as the Masters Monday Polo and the Peter Pant.

**Is Fantl Sport expensive?**
It sits at the premium end of golf apparel, above many better-known brands. Reviewers have generally argued the construction and materials justify it, though the brand does run periodic sales.

**What is the Fantl Sport design philosophy?**
The brand describes its work as considered golf utility that balances function and refined design, built to help people play and keep playing for as long as possible.

**What is One Shot at Life?**
A 2025 brand film directed by Reagan Butler through the production company Supernormal, shot in black and white and told from the perspective of the founder's grandfather. It was covered by The Drum.

**Does Fantl Sport ship internationally?**
Yes. The brand ships from New Zealand and has built an audience well beyond its domestic market.
