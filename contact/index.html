Who We Are

DORMIED is an independent golf brand intelligence platform. We track search interest across 169 golf brands globally, produce original editorial coverage, and publish the DORMIED Index — a monthly ranking of golf brand health by consumer search demand.

The platform is run by Travis (rankings, data, equipment & general coverage) and Adam (apparel, footwear, and bags coverage).

We are not affiliated with any golf brand. Rankings and editorial opinions are entirely independent.

Corrections Policy

We take accuracy seriously. If you believe something we have published contains a factual error, email us with the article URL and the specific correction. We will review it and update the article with a correction note if warranted.
