# What Is Khalhon? The Seoul Golf Brand That Got Sean Wotherspoon Into Golf

*By Adam R. | Last updated: September 8, 2026*

*Adam R. covers apparel, accessories, and golf culture for DORMIED. He has been tracking brand drops and collaborations since 2018.*

Khalhon is a Seoul golf apparel brand that grew out of the city's golf scene in the mid-2020s, sitting deliberately at the intersection of sport, streetwear and quiet luxury. Its most consequential move so far has been a capsule with Sean Wotherspoon, the Round Two co-founder and one of the most influential designers in streetwear, who had never worked in golf before. He described Khalhon as an amazing vehicle for his design work, and cheerfully admitted he is still a terrible golfer.

In one sentence: Khalhon is a Seoul-based golf apparel brand blending American casual with golf tradition, known for a Sean Wotherspoon capsule and a footwear collaboration with Payntr Golf launched at Hypegolf's SoHo pop-up.

## Why the Wotherspoon capsule matters

It would be easy to file this as another celebrity collaboration. It is not, and the distinction is worth spelling out.

Sean Wotherspoon is not an influencer who happens to like golf. He co-founded Round Two, the vintage and streetwear store that became a cultural institution, and he designed the Nike Air Max 1/97 corduroy colourway that is among the defining sneakers of the late 2010s. In design circles he is regarded as one of the more influential creative voices of his generation, particularly for how he uses nostalgia and colour.

Golf was a category he had never touched. Speaking about the Khalhon project, he sounded genuinely energised by that, describing golf as completely fresh territory and saying the brand would be an amazing vehicle for his design work. He framed his creative direction for it as disruptive, colourful, nostalgic and modern. He was equally enthusiastic about Korea itself, saying he admires Korean fashion culture, while repeatedly insisting he remains a terrible golfer.

That last part is the tell. Someone parachuting into golf because the category became fashionable does not lead with their own incompetence at it. Wotherspoon's interest reads as curiosity about what Korea might do with golf next, which is a different thing from a licensing deal. He is a collaborator on a capsule, not an owner.

## Seoul as the starting point

Korean golf fashion has been quietly ahead of the rest of the world for years, and Khalhon is one of the brands making that visible outside Korea.

The brand was founded in Seoul and grew out of the city's golf scene in the mid-2020s, designed from a desk in Hannam-dong. Its own framing is blunt: it reframes golf as culture, not performance gear, and it aims at the late-twenty- and thirty-something creative professional who treats the fairway as just another place to dress with intention.

That is a more considered position than most brands bother to articulate. The clothes follow it: clean silhouettes, considered materials, and what the brand calls a refusal to lean on tradition for credibility. Khalhon describes golfwear as reclaiming its role as a cultural language rather than a uniform, and says the goal is that a wearer feels a sense of ease and confidence that emerges naturally, without the need for self-display. The feeling should start with "this feels right for me" rather than an awareness of being well dressed.

Retail-wise, Khalhon has been deliberate rather than aggressive. Rather than simply supplying stock, the brand offers partners flexible exclusive options, adjusting colours, graphics and embroidery so a collection sits in harmony with each store's identity.

## The Payntr collaboration

Alongside Wotherspoon, Khalhon has moved into footwear through a partnership with Payntr Golf, the performance brand built around the biomechanics of the golf swing.

The two debuted an exclusive footwear and apparel collaboration at the Hypegolf Clubhouse pop-up in SoHo, which is about as precise a targeting of the golf-and-streetwear audience as exists. The limited-edition line launched on 29 August 2025 at 147 Grand Street and ran through 28 September, with Hypegolf turning the space into a month-long run of golf, culture and style, before a wider release later that autumn.

The pairing makes sense. Payntr brings the technical credibility Khalhon does not claim, and Khalhon brings the cultural positioning Payntr does not have. The stated idea is golfers who move between the course and the city, which is the same customer the brand describes on its own site.

## Why it matters

Golf's culture-brand wave has so far been mostly American, running through Malbon, Students, Metalwood and the rest. Khalhon is a reminder that Korea has its own version, arguably further along, and that the traffic is starting to run in both directions.

The Wotherspoon capsule is the clearest evidence. One of the most sought-after collaborators in streetwear chose a Seoul golf brand for his first work in the category. That is not a brand buying credibility. That is a designer going where he thinks the interesting work is.

## FAQ

**What is Khalhon?**
Khalhon is a Seoul-based golf apparel brand founded in the mid-2020s, blending American casual influences with golf tradition. It positions itself between sport, streetwear and quiet luxury, and describes what it does as reframing golf as culture rather than performance gear.

**Is Khalhon related to Sean Wotherspoon?**
Wotherspoon, co-founder of Round Two, collaborated with Khalhon on a capsule collection. It is his first work in golf. He is a collaborator rather than an owner.

**Who is Sean Wotherspoon?**
A designer and Round Two co-founder best known for the Nike Air Max 1/97 corduroy sneaker. He is widely regarded as one of the most influential streetwear designers of his generation.

**Where is Khalhon based?**
Seoul, South Korea, with design work out of Hannam-dong. The brand describes itself as designed in Seoul and worn worldwide.

**What is the Khalhon and Payntr collaboration?**
An exclusive footwear and apparel collection between Khalhon and Payntr Golf, launched 29 August 2025 at the Hypegolf Clubhouse pop-up at 147 Grand Street in SoHo, combining Payntr's performance footwear engineering with Khalhon's design direction.

**How do you pronounce and spell Khalhon?**
It is spelled Khalhon, with an h in the middle. The brand's site is khalhon.com and its Instagram is @khalhon.official. It is frequently misspelled as Khalon.

**What does Khalhon sell?**
Golf apparel built on clean silhouettes and considered materials, plus footwear through the Payntr collaboration.
